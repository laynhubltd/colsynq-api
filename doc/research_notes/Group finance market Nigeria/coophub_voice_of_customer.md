# CoopHub (Wema Bank) — voice of the customer

**Compiled:** 9 Oct 2026. **Scope:** what users, cooperative officers and commentators say about Wema Bank's CoopHub (coophub.alat.ng; Android `alat.ng.coophub`; iOS id6502437864), plus Wema/ALAT service issues that would reach CoopHub users (CoopHub money sits in Wema accounts and shares ALAT rails, KYC and support).

**Method:** ~25 web searches; direct pulls of Google Play reviews (Play's public review endpoint, sorted newest) for CoopHub (all 11 text reviews), ALAT (newest 8,000 reviews, 20 Jul 2023 – 8 Oct 2026) and ALAT for Business (all 605 reviews); Apple App Store lookup and review RSS for NG/US/GB storefronts; press articles; Wema's 2025 annual report (full-text search). Keyword counts are crude regex matches and overlap; treat them as order-of-magnitude.

**Labels:** **[snippet]** = seen only in a search-engine excerpt (page blocked or not loaded). **[self-reported]** = Wema's own claim. **[promo]** = press piece that reads as sponsored or reproduces a press release. **[unverified]** = single source, not corroborated.

**Access blocks:** Nairaland returned HTTP 403 (thread comments unread); Reddit returned 403 to automated search; Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn require login; X/Twitter and TikTok were reachable only through search snippets; coophub.alat.ng terms page is client-rendered and returned no readable text. YouTube was not searched directly beyond general web search (no CoopHub videos surfaced).

---

## 1. Headline

1. **There is almost no independent public voice of the customer for CoopHub.** Across app stores, forums, social media and press, the total body of user-authored CoopHub content found is **13 short app-store reviews** (11 Google Play, 2 Apple NG). No Nairaland, Reddit, X, Facebook or press complaint about CoopHub itself was found.
2. **What exists is launch-skewed and possibly insider-skewed.** 6 of 11 Play reviews were posted 10–16 May 2024 (launch week), all 5-star. One launch-day 5-star reviewer shares a name with Wema's Lead Product Designer, Innovation (LinkedIn) [name match, identity unverified].
3. **Post-launch reviews are mixed and point at onboarding and exit**, not at features: "how do I get default password" (May 2024), "how do I create cooperative profile" (Feb 2025), and an unwanted-account complaint that the app is "refusing to close" (Sep 2026). **None got a developer reply** (0 of 11).
4. **Adoption claim is old and unverified.** "Over 1,000 cooperative societies" onboarded is a single Wema statement from July 2024, repeated verbatim by press [self-reported]. No later figure appears in press or in Wema's 2025 annual report, which mentions CoopHub once, in a list of products. Android installs are only **1K+** (Oct 2026), and both apps were **last updated Oct 2025** (v1.1.6) — about 12 months without a release.
5. **The parent channel is where the pain is.** ALAT Google Play ratings fell from 3.55 avg (2024) to 2.99 (2026), with 1–2-star share rising from 32% to 47%. Dominant complaints: KYC/NIN/BVN/tier upgrades, login/OTP/face capture, network failures, slow support, account restrictions (PND). A Jan 2026 ALAT relaunch triggered a review spike (Feb 2026 avg 2.55). Wema also had a core-banking failure on 16 Jan 2025 (₦2.9bn moved without authorisation) and suspended its X (7 Jun 2026) and Telegram (29 Jun 2026) presence over impersonation fraud — removing a public complaint channel.

## 6. Comparisons, choice and churn

- **No public comparison by users** of CoopHub vs spreadsheets or rival cooperative software (Coopify, Coopco, ProbityCoop, Coopra, SmartCoopHub, CrediCircle). Comparisons exist only in vendor marketing and in our own landscape note (`competitive_landscape.md`).
- **Why chosen (inferred from Wema messaging, not users):** free/bank-backed, three-party withdrawal approval, loans, member app. No user explicitly cites "free" or "bank-backed".
- **Churn / abandonment evidence:** weak but present — (a) the Sep 2026 review asks to close an account the user did not want; (b) installs remain 1K+ two years after a claimed 1,000+ cooperatives; (c) both apps unchanged since Oct 2025; (d) no updated adoption figure since Jul 2024. None of these is proof of churn; together they suggest low or stalled active use.

## 9. Gaps

- **Nairaland thread comments** (403) and **Reddit** (403) not readable; Facebook/Instagram/LinkedIn behind login; X only via snippets. A logged-in manual pass of Facebook cooperative groups and WhatsApp-adjacent channels is the most likely place for real CoopHub talk.
- **No interviews or surveys** of cooperative officers exist publicly; the most valuable next step is 5–10 direct calls to Lagos staff cooperatives that attended the Jul 2024 Wema event.
- **No pricing, T&Cs, fee schedule or loan terms** retrieved (coophub.alat.ng is client-rendered).
- **No active-use metric** (active societies, members, contribution volume) from Wema in press, annual report or app stores. iOS install count unavailable.
- **Keyword counts** on ALAT reviews are regex-based, overlap, and cover Google Play only (not iOS). The 8,000-review sample covers Jul 2023 – Oct 2026, not the full review history.
- **Reviewer identity** (possible Wema staff) is a name match only.
- No FCCPC, CBN or consumer-complaint-site entries naming CoopHub were found; this is absence of evidence, not proof of no complaints.
